The Crew

Michael De Santa

The Retired Bank Robber

A retired bank robber living a life of luxury in Vinewood Hills. He is struggling with a family in crisis and a life of crime that won't let him go.

Franklin Clinton

The Street Hustler

A street hustler looking for real opportunities and serious money. He works for a car dealer, but feels like he's destined for more.

Trevor Philips

The Psychopath

A volatile and unpredictable career criminal. He is Michael's old partner in crime and lives in a trailer in the desert.

Jimmy De Santa

A Hardcore Gamer

Jimmy De Santa, the lazy and entitled son of Michael in Grand Theft Auto V. He is ungrateful attitude, video game obsession, and strained relationship with his father.

Lester Crest

The Master Planner

Lester Crest is the mastermind planner of Grand Theft Auto V, known for his cynical humor, obsession with 1980s pop culture, and high-level intellect despite suffering from a wasting disease.

About The Game

Grand Theft Auto V is an action-adventure game played from a third-person perspective. Players complete missions—linear scenarios with set objectives—to progress through the story. Outside of the missions, players may freely roam the open world.

Set in the fictional state of San Andreas, based on Southern California, the game features a massive and diverse environment.
